## Why only twelve and not sixty

The plan was to take sixty new heads of business. What actually happened:

1. **Bulk data does not give staff names.** The government's GIAS download
   needs an interactive, token-protected form POST; my scripted attempt returned
   the page rather than the zip. Even with it, GIAS has school websites, not
   department emails.
2. **Search engines block scripted harvesting.** DuckDuckGo's lite endpoint
   returns a bot page (HTTP 202) to every scripted request; Mojeek 403s. So
   `sales/harvest_business_contacts.py` runs but harvests nothing — the file is
   kept as the record of the attempt, not as a working tool.
3. **The manual route yields about two usable addresses per search.** Most
   staff pages either omit emails entirely, ask parents to use a contact form,
   or publish only a general office inbox. Each remaining address cost one
   search plus a read of the snippet.

Twelve verified department addresses in the time available is the honest result.
The route to sixty is roughly thirty more searches at the same yield, or a paid
staff-contact dataset — not something to fake.
